# Shadow Isaac practice policy: P0 v1

Selected on 2026-10-07 as the operating contract for a future forward **practice** cohort. This is not evidence of an edge or permission to trade. A1 records remain `practice_draft_unarmed`; the present API validates plan shape and source facts but does **not** enforce the universe, daily cap, clock schedule, or paper cost accounting below. No A1 record is prospectively eligible merely because it carries A1's `practice-long-15m-v1` version/hash. That hash covers only the A1 plan schema. The complete operating policy is separately named `shadow-isaac-p0-v1`. Before any A2 arm, the service must encode **all** eligibility, calendar, source, execution and cost rules in a canonical policy specification; hash and store that specification with the arm event, check the frozen A1 record against it, and reject or label older/noncompliant drafts instead of retroactively granting eligibility. A2 must enforce the same version at replay. Any change creates a new complete policy version/hash.

## Eligibility and daily schedule

- **A1 plan schema:** `practice-long-15m-v1`; **complete P0 operating policy:** `shadow-isaac-p0-v1`. Long underlying shares only. A regular-session 15-minute bar must close **strictly above** a frozen raw-price trigger. The plan has one fixed stop below the trigger, one fixed target above it, an entry guard, and a maximum hold of two trading sessions, counting the entry session. No options, shorts, discretionary stop moves, or real orders.
- **Universe:** SPY, QQQ, IWM, AAPL, MSFT. These are five predeclared symbols, including symbols with no setup. No substitution after seeing the day's moves. At most three TAKE candidates per session and one plan per symbol. WAIT, SKIP and no response remain visible in the denominator.
- **Preparation:** by 08:50 America/New_York on a day the verified market calendar says open. Freeze the context and commit TAKE/WAIT/SKIP by 09:00; late records remain labeled late and outside that day's on-time cohort. A context provider failure permits WAIT/SKIP, never an armed-capable TAKE. Do not infer market hours from weekdays alone.
- **Watch:** regular session only, from the first completed 09:30–09:45 bar through the last 15-minute close at or before the earlier of 15:15 ET or 45 minutes before the calendar close. That cutoff is the untriggered entry expiry. An early close moves it earlier; a closed or unavailable calendar prevents arming. No trigger after expiry enters paper. Stop/target and the two-session time exit continue after entry under the calendar's actual close.
- **Source:** completed Alpaca stock minute bars requested with `adjustment=raw`, with feed (`iex` or `sip`), UTC bar start, formation time (start plus one minute), capture time, USD/share price unit, and raw split basis in the frozen A1 envelope. For this first cohort use `freshness_limit_seconds=86400`; every referenced fact must satisfy A1's cutoff and current-time checks. IEX coverage is thinner than SIP. A future split or source-basis change requires an explicit invalidation/review, not arithmetic across bases.

## Frozen execution and cost rules

**Watch and paper source (A2):** triggers and paper fills are judged on Tradier consolidated one-minute bars (`tradier_timesales_1min`), the feed the level alert monitor already reads, and every paper event records that source. The plan's prices remain the frozen A1 Alpaca facts; consolidated and IEX prices for these five symbols differ by cents, and consolidated bars rarely omit a regular-session minute. Execution version `p0-exec-v1` adds: a trigger detected more than **300 seconds** after its 15-minute bar closed is a missed trigger; an untriggered plan expires 300 seconds after its expiry; an entry open below the guard minimum is refused like one above its maximum; a bar opening above the target fills at the target less slippage; a split during the hold leaves the outcome unresolved. The complete specification and its hash are `POLICY_SPEC` and `POLICY_HASH` in `backend/app/engine/paper.py`; arming also enforces the universe, the three-per-session and one-per-symbol caps, a same-day decision, an open calendar day and an expiry no later than the watch cutoff below.

The A2 watcher will durably accept a 15-minute close above the trigger and record condition time separately from detection time. It will make the simulated order eligible for the first **full** one-minute bar starting strictly after durable detection; it will read that bar after completion and use its open. An open through the stop, above the entry guard, or at/above the target rejects the entry. A monitoring gap that makes this timing unknowable is a missed trigger, never a favorable backdated entry.

Cost version `p0-cost-v1`: on each entry and exit, adverse slippage is **1 basis point of that side's reference price plus $0.01 per share**; commission and other fees are set to $0 for this practice model, with that omission disclosed. Long entry adds slippage; long exit subtracts it. Show a second result using triple both slippage parameters (`p0-cost-v1-x3`) without changing the base outcome. One share is the reporting unit; no capital/P&L claim follows from it. The frozen A1 `initial_risk_per_share` is **trigger level minus stop**, a positive planned-risk unit. Net planned R is (simulated exit fill minus simulated entry fill) divided by that frozen unit, for base and triple costs alike. Show simulated fill-to-stop exposure separately because a delayed entry can increase it; reject an entry if that exposure is nonpositive. Do not overwrite the frozen A1 risk after a fill. A gap through stop exits at the worse open with adverse slippage. A bar touching stop and target resolves stop first and is flagged ambiguous. A target touch fills no better than target. The time exit uses the second session's final regular one-minute close with adverse exit slippage; missing bars make the outcome unresolved. These conventions are proposed for A2 and are not calculated by A1.

## A2 recovery and verification boundaries

The A2 arm API serializes eligibility checks and the armed-event commit so concurrent requests cannot exceed the session or active-symbol caps. PostgreSQL uses a transaction advisory lock; SQLite takes its write lock before reading eligibility. A watcher pauses records whose saved complete policy hash or execution version differs from the running implementation. Supporting older versions requires an explicit compatible watcher; their stored economic events remain unchanged.

After the trigger commit returns, the watcher samples the clock and persists one `order_intent` event declaring the next full eligible minute. The trigger row's pre-commit `recorded_at` is not used as proof of durability. Restart replay uses the saved intent; a trigger with no intent after a gap longer than the permitted detection delay becomes unresolved instead of receiving a backdated or indefinitely delayed entry.

Unknown or stale split metadata pauses entry/exit evaluation. Every completed 15-minute interval evaluated before the first crossing needs complete one-minute coverage; missing intervals or unavailable historical calendars pause evaluation rather than producing a trigger or a quiet expiry. Completed regular-session entry/exit bars can be recovered after the market closes, with late reconstruction marked. Today resolves a phone-linked decision by ID even when it has aged out of the recent list. Use **Refresh paper timeline** to retrieve new events while the card stays open; the outcomes show simulated entry-to-stop exposure separately from planned R.

These behaviors have fixture regression coverage in `backend/tests/test_paper_plans.py` and a phone-link browser check in `frontend/e2e/practice-decisions.spec.ts`. PostgreSQL concurrency is additionally covered by `backend/tests/test_postgres_parity.py` when a disposable test database is supplied. None proves real provider coverage, phone receipt, or a production restart. A2 acceptance still requires the controlled live lifecycle and restart drill described in the roadmap.
