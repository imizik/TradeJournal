# QQQ 5-minute opening range breakout with tape fills: result (2026-11-14)

Protocol: [`research/qqq-orb/protocol.json`](../qqq-orb/protocol.json) (`qqq-orb-20261114-v1`), registered in #813 before any result of this rule on QQQ was computed. It ran once on 2026-11-14 with the script as merged in #813. Aggregates are in [`results.json`](../qqq-orb/results.json).

The rule is copied from Zarattini and Aziz (2023). The first five-minute candle sets the direction (up candle long, down candle short). Entry is at 09:35. The stop is the candle's far end. The target is 10 R. Anything still open closes at 15:59. Every fill comes from the consolidated tape and quotes, one second after the order. From 2016-01-04 to 2026-11-13 there were 2,712 sessions after leaving out 21 early closes, which gave 2,690 trades. Of the rest, 19 were dojis, 2 were missing bars and 1 had no entry quote.

## Decision: no evidence either way

| Trades | Mean net R a trade | 99% interval | 2016-2019 / 2020-2023-02 / 2023-02-2026 | Extra cent a side |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,690 | +0.090R | -0.020R to +0.202R | +0.155R / +0.044R / +0.059R | +0.033R |

"Worth a paper test" needed three things: the interval's lower end above zero, every period above zero, and the extra-cent mean above zero. Every period and the extra-cent mean were above zero. The interval's lower end was not, so under the protocol the 5-minute QQQ opening range breakout is closed. No variants are to be tried on any history: no other range lengths, stops, targets, symbols or filters.

This is the closest any of the 18 opening breakout attempts has come. The mean was positive after real spreads in all three periods, including the one after the paper's publication. But the paper's own sample (2016 to early 2023) makes up most of the evidence. On the 99% standard set for an 18th attempt, the result cannot be told apart from zero.

## What else the run shows (descriptive, not tested)

- **The paper replicates on bars:** the paper's version on one-minute bars (entry at P, no costs) averaged +0.154R with a 24.4% win rate. The paper reports +0.13R and 24%.
- **Real fills cost about 0.06R a trade:** going from bar prices to tape fills took the mean from +0.154R to +0.090R. The entry spread alone averaged 0.036R (median 0.025R). The rest is stop slippage and the bid/ask at the close.
- **After publication it is weaker:** +0.059R a trade from 2023-02-21, against +0.155R in 2016-2019. By year: 2023 -0.04R, 2024 +0.01R, 2025 +0.16R, 2026 +0.02R. 2017 was the only other losing year (-0.08R).
- **The 10 R target hardly matters:** only 65 of 2,690 trades (2.4%) reached it. 2,004 stopped out (74.5%) and 621 closed at 15:59. With no target, the same tape rules averaged +0.101R. The edge, if any, comes from the few days that trend all day.
- **Longs and shorts are alike:** +0.090R long (1,363 trades), +0.089R short (1,327).
- **The typical trade loses a full R:** the median trade was -1.02R. A year of this rule is roughly 185 stop-outs, with the gain resting on about 60 trend days.
- **It is not one lucky month:** without the best 1% of months the mean was +0.077R.
- **Wide first candles did worse:** the smallest fifth of R (3 to 24 cents) averaged +0.13R and the largest fifth (127 to 604 cents) +0.02R. The small-R trades pay the most spread relative to R, yet still did better.

## Limits

- Fills assume the displayed best quote one second after the order. Real routing can differ.
- The 09:30 bar's open is the first consolidated trade, not necessarily the official opening auction price.
- The close is a market order at 15:59 at the quote, not the closing auction.
- With tiny first candles, a cent of spread is a large part of R. Neither the paper nor this test sets a minimum.
