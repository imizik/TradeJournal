# Why three morning briefs missed the 09:00 movers

None of the 20 reconstructed movers appeared by ticker in the frozen packet
for any of the three briefs. All three packets closed around 08:45 ET. The
zero overlap therefore arose before the writer selected stories. On one
morning the writer also passed over candidates that *were* supplied, but
those candidates were outside the 09:00 top ten on either side.

## Three sessions, selected by time alone

From the [scorecard census and baseline supplement](morning-brief-scorecard.md),
take the earliest three distinct session dates with an unredacted version
saved before 09:30 ET. Within a date take the earliest eligible save. Each
selected date had one version. The two October 19 saves were both late, so
neither contributes a session. “Saved” means available in the journal.

| Session (2026) | Packet as of → saved, ET | What the writer had | What the published brief covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 20 | 08:45:18 → 09:26:56 | 20 price observations, six dated that morning, and four headlines. No discovery list. | Two priorities addressed expiry risk and a saved price level. Two tickers named, 0/20 movers. |
| Oct 21 | 08:45:18 → 08:56:52 | 20 price observations, all from earlier ET dates, and eight headlines. No discovery list or broad market backdrop. | Two priorities addressed near-term risk and price confirmation. Four tickers named, 0/20 movers. The final 09:00 list was still in the future. |
| Oct 22 | 08:45:34 → 09:05:11 | 27 same-morning price observations, eight symbol headlines, a macro backdrop and six news-led discovery candidates. None of the six was a baseline mover. | The summary used the backdrop. All three priority items cited position evidence. Two discovery names appeared only in limitations and none got a priority item. Eleven tickers named, 0/20 movers. |

The October 20 and 22 saves followed the cutoff, so the market observations
through 08:59 could by then have existed. Neither packet refreshed between
08:45 and publication. All three lists were reconstructed later from SIP
minutes and date-effective listings. There is no contemporaneous 09:00 ranking
in these inputs, or earlier whole-market ranking to substitute on October 21.

## What the evidence can establish

We checked each private daily archive's first ten gainers and ten losers
against the scorecard baseline. For each saved version we joined its original
packet and searched for those exact ticker tokens across all evidence IDs,
text, sources, scope and gaps. None matched and none of the packets referenced
separate memory revisions. For output we used the scorecard's manually reviewed
visible mentions keyed to the complete saved record, then checked the item,
summary and limitation sections separately for the six discovery candidates.
Counts are per version and include incidental visible mentions.

All three records identify the same v2 prompt. Its mandate was to prioritize
up to three personally relevant developments from frozen evidence. Expiry or
level risk qualified. On October 22, leaving six supplied candidates out of
the priority items was an editorial choice. The evidence does not establish
that any deserved a slot. Applying the later opportunity-first instructions to
these saves would misstate the assignment. [Brief mandate and version history](../../docs/agent/dots-integration.md#personalized-morning-briefing-and-memory)

These packets preserve a bounded news sample. They cannot establish whether
another source had already reported a mover's catalyst, whether an issuer was
mentioned only by company name, or how useful the selected stories were.
The smallest next check is to compare an existing, independently timestamped
source-report archive with one of these packets at its **08:45 freeze**. Count
how many baseline movers had a report by then and how many reports reached the
packet. Until that evidence is available, catalyst coverage is unknown.
