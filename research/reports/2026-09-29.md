## What the ledger says now

Everything tried so far has failed, and for two distinct reasons. The two intraday 1-minute patterns (VWAP reclaim and the failed breakout) do pick entries better than random coin-flip entries, but their stops are so tight that the per-trade cost of about 0.06-0.08R is larger than the edge, so they end up losing money; the fix has to be fewer trades with more dollars of room each, not another target or hold tweak on 1-minute bars. The swing pattern (recovery swing) is the only one that makes money before the bar, but its margin over random entries is too small to be distinguishable from luck, so it needs a cleaner trigger or a rule that removes its worst trades rather than a new exit. Meta-labeling with a logistic regression on all ten features did not rescue it: it improved the average but never got near the significance bar and lost most of its improvement when costs were tripled. Discovery slices point at two weak spots in the swing: trades triggered on the very first bar of the session are badly negative, and trades taken while SPY is down on the day are negative. The confirmation bar is now t >= 2.61 and rises with each new arrival, so only ideas with a mechanical reason to work, not a fitted number, are worth sending.

## Building blocks wanted

- A trailing or breakeven-stop exit (e.g. move the stop to entry after +1R, or trail by N ATR), so we can test whether the losing tail rather than the entry is what kills these patterns
- An earnings/event proximity feature or filter (sessions until the next scheduled report), since overnight holds in the swing family straddle earnings
- A volatility-regime feature such as the ticker's daily ATR percentile against its own past year, to separate 'more room per trade' from 'noisier tape'
- A distance-from-VWAP (in ATR) feature at the signal bar, so VWAP-family ideas can be filtered by how extended the confirmation is instead of only by the built-in chase_atr
- A limit or pullback entry option (fill on a retrace toward the trigger level rather than at the signal bar's close), which would cut the cost drag that sank both 1-minute families
- A per-trade cost report in R by family and timeframe, so we can see directly how much room a trade needs before costs stop mattering
